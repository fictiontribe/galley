This site is a set of plain pages. It sets no cookies of its own and loads nothing from other companies' servers.

## Counting visits

If visit counting is switched on, it waits until you say yes in the banner. Saying no, or saying nothing, means nothing is counted. You can change your answer from the "Privacy choices" link at any time.

## The newsletter and the contact form

When you subscribe or write, your address and message are kept so they can be answered and the letter can be sent. They are never sold or shared. Every letter has a one-click way to stop.

## AI crawlers

Each page is also published in plain Markdown for AI tools and search systems, and the site's `robots.txt` says which crawlers may read it. See [llms.txt](/llms.txt).
