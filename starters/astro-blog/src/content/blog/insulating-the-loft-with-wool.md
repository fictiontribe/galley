The loft had about 80 mm of old fibreglass, yellowed and squashed, with a cold strip all round the edge where it never reached the wall. Current guidance is 270 mm or more. I wanted to go to 300 mm over the top of what was there.

On cost alone, mineral wool wins and it isn't close. I chose wool for two reasons: I was doing it myself, in June, under a hot roof, and the thought of two days in a mask with glass fibres down my sleeves decided it; and the roof has no membrane under the slates, so the loft gets damp air in winter, which wool takes up and gives back without losing much warmth.

> [!CAUTION]
> I nearly packed the eaves solid. Without the air gap under the tiles, warm damp air from the house condenses on the cold roof timbers. Eaves trays cost £2 each. Use them.
