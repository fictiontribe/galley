The three back windows were the last single glazing in the house. Replacing them with double-glazed timber sashes was quoted at £9,000. A slim aluminium secondary pane on the inside of each was £1,150 for all three.

The rooms behind are quieter too, which I hadn't thought about. The only change in how we live is a small one: on cold nights the old outer glass mists over, so I leave the top sash open a finger's width to let it dry.
