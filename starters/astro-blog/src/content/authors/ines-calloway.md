I started Sarking to keep myself honest. When you spend money on a house, it is easy to remember the good numbers and forget the rest. So everything goes here: the readings, the bills, the quotes I turned down and the jobs I would do differently.

The house is a three-bedroom semi, built in 1934 with solid brick walls, a hipped tile roof and a bay at the front. When I moved in it used about 19,000 kWh a year. The aim was a third of that without making it a worse place to live.

I am not an installer and I sell nothing. Where a post leans on someone else's knowledge, it says so.

*Ines is invented for this template, as is the house.*
