---
title: Certificate expiry forecasts
date: 2026-10-28
version: "4.5"
tags: [New, Improved]
---

The certificate register now forecasts renewals for the next eighteen months, by site and by contractor, so you can book renewals in quiet weeks rather than the week before they lapse.

- A new **Forecast** view groups upcoming expiries by month.
- Expiry reminders can now go to the contractor as well as the site owner.
- Certificates uploaded as photos are read automatically, and the expiry date is suggested for you to confirm.
