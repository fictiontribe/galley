---
title: QR labels for every asset
date: 2026-07-22
version: "4.1"
tags: [New, Improved]
---

Print a QR label for any asset from its page. Scanning it with a phone camera opens the asset's history and a form to report a fault — no app needed for the person reporting.

- Labels print on standard sheets, 24 to a page.
- Fault reports from a label create a work order with the asset and site already filled in.
- Improved: the asset page now shows the next three due tasks at the top.
