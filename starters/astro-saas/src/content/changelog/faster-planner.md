title: A faster planner for large estates
date: 2026-10-09
version: "4.4"
tags: [Improved]
---

The planner now opens in under a second on estates with more than 500 sites, and scrolling through a year of tasks no longer pauses to load.

- Filters for region, asset type and owner are remembered between visits.
- Dragging a task to another week now moves its follow-up tasks with it, if you ask it to.
