The Ferrow app for Android and iOS now works fully offline. Before heading out, the app downloads each person's inspections for the next seven days, with their checklists, asset history and reference photos.

- Answers, readings, photos and signatures are saved on the phone and sent when it finds a connection.
- A banner shows how many inspections are waiting to sync, and when the last sync happened.
- If two people edit the same inspection, Ferrow keeps both versions and asks a manager to choose.

Offline mode is on for every plan. Update the app to version 4.6 to use it.
