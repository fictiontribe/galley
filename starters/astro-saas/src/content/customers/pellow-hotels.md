Pellow Hotels runs seventeen hotels with a small central facilities team and more than sixty local contractors — plumbers, electricians, lift engineers and kitchen specialists.

## A job is only as fast as its last email

When a guest reported a broken shower, the front desk emailed facilities, facilities emailed a contractor, and the contractor replied — eventually. "Half of my week was chasing," says Teodor Rask, Pellow's facilities lead. "Is it booked? Is it done? Where's the invoice?"

## Contractors in the loop, without another login

Pellow invited every contractor to the Ferrow portal. Contractors receive the job with the room, the asset and its history, update it from their phone and attach photos and the invoice when it is done. There is nothing to install and nothing for them to pay.

## What changed

- **Reactive jobs close 34% faster** than in the year before.
- **61 contractors update their own jobs**, so the facilities team stopped chasing.
- **Costs are captured per hotel and per job**, ready for month-end.
