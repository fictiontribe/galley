Wendmoor Senior Living runs 38 residences for older people. Water hygiene — flushing little-used outlets, checking temperatures, cleaning showerheads — is one of the most important routines in every building, and one of the easiest to fall behind on.

## Paper logs on every corridor

Each residence kept paper flushing logs on clipboards. Managers collected them at the end of the month and typed them into a spreadsheet. Gaps were found weeks after they happened.

## The same checks, on every phone

Wendmoor loaded all 11,400 water outlets into Ferrow, each with its location and flushing interval. Housekeeping and maintenance staff now see each day's outlets on a shared phone and record temperatures as they go. A reading outside the safe range raises a work order straight away.

## What changed

- **Managers spend half as long on inspection paperwork**, because the records build themselves.
- **Every residence runs the same checklists**, so the property team compares like with like.
- **Gaps show up the same day**, not at the end of the month.
