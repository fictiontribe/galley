## This website

You may read, link to and share the pages on this site. The text, product images and logos belong to Ferrow; please do not reuse them to suggest you are us or that we endorse you.

We work to keep the information here accurate, but prices, features and plans can change. The figures shown in customer stories describe those customers' results; yours will depend on your estate.

## Subscriptions

Ferrow is sold as an annual or monthly subscription, priced per site. Your order form sets out the plan, the number of sites and the price; our customer agreement sets out everything else, including:

- **Availability** — our uptime commitment and what happens if we miss it.
- **Your data** — that it remains yours, how we protect it, and how you can export it.
- **Support** — the hours and response times for your plan.
- **Ending the contract** — how to give notice, and what happens to your data afterwards.

Where the customer agreement and this page differ, the customer agreement wins.
