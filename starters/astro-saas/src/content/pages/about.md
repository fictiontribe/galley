  - type: rich-text

    heading: Why Ferrow exists
    text: |
      Hana Lindqvist ran facilities for a group of clinics. Every Monday she opened six spreadsheets, three inboxes and a wall planner to work out what was due that week — and every quarter something slipped through anyway.

      Tobi Merriden had built scheduling software for airlines. Over a long lunch he asked her a simple question: *why is the plan in one place and the proof in another?*

      Ferrow is the answer they built together. **One schedule, the evidence attached to the work, and nothing that depends on one person remembering.** Today it runs planned maintenance and inspections for 380 organizations, from cold-chain depots to primary schools.
