_Last updated 1 November 2026. Ferrow is a fictional company; this policy is sample text for a template and is not legal advice. Have yours written for your own business._

## Who we are

Ferrow ("we") makes software for planned maintenance, inspections and compliance. This policy covers this website and the Ferrow product. Questions go to [privacy@example.com](mailto:privacy@example.com).

## What we collect

**When you visit this website**, we keep standard server logs — the page requested, the time and your browser's details — for 30 days, to keep the site running and secure. This site sets no advertising or tracking cookies and loads nothing from third parties.

**When you contact us or book a demo**, we keep your name, work email, company and what you wrote, so we can reply and follow up. We keep it for two years after our last conversation, unless you become a customer.

**When your organization uses Ferrow**, we process the information your team puts into it — names and work email addresses of users, and the photos, readings and notes recorded during inspections. Your organization decides what goes in; we process it on their instructions, under our customer agreement.

## How we use it

- To provide and support the product your organization pays for.
- To answer your questions and arrange demos you ask for.
- To send the monthly email, only if you subscribed. Every email has a one-click unsubscribe.

We never sell personal information and never use customer data to train models.

## Who we share it with

We use a small number of service providers — hosting, email delivery and support tools — each bound by a contract that limits what they may do with your information. We publish the current list and give customers 30 days' notice before adding to it.

## Your choices

You can ask to see, correct, export or delete the personal information we hold about you by writing to [privacy@example.com](mailto:privacy@example.com). If you use Ferrow through your employer, we will pass your request to them, as they control that data.

## Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will say so on this page and, for customers, by email before the change takes effect.
