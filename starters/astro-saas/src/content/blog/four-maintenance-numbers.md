We are often asked to add another chart to Ferrow's dashboard. Usually we say no. A dashboard that shows everything helps nobody decide anything. These are the four numbers we think matter most.

## 1. Statutory checks done on time

The share of legally required checks completed by their due date, over the last 90 days. This is the number your board and your insurer care about. Keep it separate from routine tasks, or a good month on light-bulb changes will hide a bad month on fire safety.

## 2. Overdue work, by age

Not just how many tasks are overdue, but for how long. Ten tasks a day late is a busy week; two tasks ninety days late is a risk. Show the oldest items by name.

## 3. Reactive jobs closed within their response time

For reactive work, the share of jobs closed within the time your contract promises. It tells you about your contractors and your own triage at the same time.

## 4. Planned versus reactive spend

The ratio of money spent on planned maintenance to money spent fixing things that broke. Over time, a healthy estate shifts spending towards planned work. If the ratio moves the other way, your plan is not keeping up with your buildings.

## What we leave off

Counts of tasks completed, logins, photos uploaded — they rise when the team is busy, whether or not the estate is safer. Measure outcomes, not activity.
