## What this site collects

Nothing on its own. There are no cookies and no tracking scripts, and the pages load nothing from anywhere else. If we ever count visits, a banner will ask you first, and nothing is counted until you say yes. The film opens on another site only when you choose to play it.

## When you write to us

What you send through a form on this site — your name, your address and your message — goes to our inbox and nowhere else. We keep it as long as we need it to answer you and to look after anything you have bought, and we never share it. If you would rather, write to studio@example.com from your own mail.

## Our letters

If you ask for our four letters a year, we keep your email address for that and nothing else. Every letter has a way to stop them. Ask, and we will delete the address the same day.

## Your rights

You can ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct it or delete it, at studio@example.com. If you are unhappy with how we have handled it, you can complain to the data protection authority where you live.
