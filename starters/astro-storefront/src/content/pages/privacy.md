## What this site collects

Very little. There are no tracking scripts, and the pages load nothing from anywhere else until you do something that needs it. If we ever count visits, a banner will ask you first, and nothing is counted until you say yes. The film opens on another site only when you choose to play it.

## The bag

What you put in the bag is kept in your own browser (its local storage), so it is still there when you come back. It never leaves your device in the demo shop. Once the shop is connected to our store, the bag is also kept by the store that sells for us, so that it can turn into an order; when you first touch a page, the site asks the store for the latest prices and what is left. Clearing your browser's storage empties the bag.

## Checkout

You pay on the store's own checkout pages, not on this site. What you type there — your address, your card — goes to the store and its payment service, under the store's privacy notice, which the checkout links to. We see your order so that we can make and send it. When you answer the banner about counting visits, your answer is passed to the store too, so its checkout respects it.

## When you write to us

What you send through a form on this site — your name, your address and your message — goes to our inbox and nowhere else. We keep it as long as we need it to answer you and to look after anything you have bought, and we never share it. If you would rather, write to studio@example.com from your own mail.

## Our letters

If you ask for our four letters a year, we keep your email address for that and nothing else. Every letter has a way to stop them. Ask, and we will delete the address the same day.

## Your rights

You can ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct it or delete it, at studio@example.com. If you are unhappy with how we have handled it, you can complain to the data protection authority where you live.
