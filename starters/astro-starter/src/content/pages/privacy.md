This site does not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers. It is a set of plain pages, and nothing is recorded about your visit beyond the standard logs our web host keeps for security.

## What we collect

When you email us or use the contact form, we receive:

- your name and email address;
- anything you choose to tell us about your home and project.

We use that information to reply to you and, if we work together, to run your project. We never sell it or share it with anyone outside the studio, except the consultants on your project when you have agreed to it.

## How long we keep it

Enquiries that do not turn into projects are deleted after twelve months. Project records are kept for fifteen years, because building work can raise questions long after it is finished.

## Your rights

You can ask to see the information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Write to hello@example.com and we will reply within a month.
