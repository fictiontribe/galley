  - type: rich-text
    eyebrow: Why we started
    heading: Good design should not need a big budget
    text: |-
      Lowfield began in 2013 at a kitchen table on Mill Lane, when Nell Okafor left a large practice in the city to work closer to home. Her neighbours kept asking the same question: *is it worth getting an architect for a job this size?*

      We think it is, as long as the architect respects the size of the job. That means fixed fees, drawings a builder can price from, and advice that is honest about cost. It also means telling people when they do not need us at all.

      Twelve years later we are four people in a converted tannery, and most of our clients still live within a few miles of the studio.
