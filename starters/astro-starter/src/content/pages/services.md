  - type: faq
    eyebrow: Questions
    heading: Things people ask at the first visit
    items:
      - question: Do I need planning permission?
        answer: It depends on the house, the location and the size of the change. Many rear extensions and loft conversions fall under *permitted development*, but conservation areas, listed buildings and flats have stricter rules. We will tell you which route applies at the first visit.
      - question: How long does it all take?
        answer: For a typical extension, allow two to three months for design, eight weeks for a planning decision, and four to six months on site. Garden rooms are quicker; listed buildings are slower.
      - question: Can you recommend a builder?
        answer: We can introduce you to builders we have worked with and trust, and we always suggest getting at least three prices. We never take a fee or commission from a contractor.
      - question: What if I only want drawings?
        answer: That is fine. Our planning-only package covers survey, design and the application itself. You can come back to us for technical drawings later if you need them.
