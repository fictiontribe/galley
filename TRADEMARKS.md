## You may

- Use "Galley" to refer to this project and its software, in documentation,

- Say, truthfully, that your site, adapter, plugin or service **works with

- Name an adapter package `galley-adapter-<framework>`, as the adapter guide
  recommends, as long as its description makes clear it is yours and works
  *with* Galley.
- Run Galley unmodified, for yourself, your team or your clients, under its

## Please do not

- Use "Galley" as, or as part of, the name of your own product, company,
  domain, npm package (other than an adapter named as above) or social

- Use the name or logo in a way that suggests Fiction Tribe endorses,
  certifies or is affiliated with your product, organisation or fork when it
  isn't.
- Modify the logo, or combine it with another mark.
- Ship a modified fork under the Galley name or logo. Forks are welcome under
  the licence; please rename them.
- Offer a hosted or modified Galley to others under the Galley name and logo.
  Galley is white-labelable for exactly this reason: a brand file (or
  `GALLEY_BRAND_NAME`) gives a deployment its own name, colour and logo without
  touching the code. "Built with Galley" in its documentation is welcome.
