## After the independent check (2026-11-25)

30 statements confirmed; two quotations were not on their pages as visible text (rows 12 and 14, replaced), seven statements said more than their source and one had none; all corrected. Galley's side: the built-in assistant (not every AI tool) is the one that never publishes; the MCP publish switch is named; Build mode is mentioned; image generation uses a key for the image service you choose; the article now says where Sanity, Contentful, Storyblok and Hygraph are the better choice (AI translation, multi-language work).

After the coordinator's check (2026-11-25): the "same place" paragraph says Hygraph's agent runs over MCP land in draft and Strapi's Review Workflows are on its Enterprise plan, and adds that most of these products' MCP servers can also publish when asked, as Galley's can unless that is switched off; the Claude answer adds that Galley doesn't check that you asked.
