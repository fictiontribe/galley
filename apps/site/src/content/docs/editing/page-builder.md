For people who edit a site. **Arrange** lets you put a page together from the sections the site already has, on the page itself.

Every move is one small change to the page, waiting in Proof like any other.

## Arrange a page

1. Open the page in the editor and choose **Arrange** above the page. **Show the Arrange panel** opens the panel beside it.
2. **Select** a section by clicking it. Its name and place appear (**Photo and text 1/8**), with a toolbar: drag handle, move up, move down, duplicate, remove, **Options** and **Edit content**.

3. **Move** a section by dragging its handle; a line shows where it will land. **Escape** leaves it where it was.
4. **Options** — tone, alignment, which side the picture is on — are switches in the panel. The page updates a moment after each one.
5. **Add a section**: drag its picture from the panel onto the page, or pick one and then choose its place. **Find a section type…** narrows the list.
6. **Edit content** opens the section's words and pictures in the form.

Every move, duplicate and removal offers **Undo**. Nothing goes live until you publish.

### From the keyboard

Tab to the page's sections, ↑ and ↓ to choose one, Alt+↑ / Alt+↓ to move it, Delete to remove it, Enter to edit its content, and ⌘Z (Ctrl+Z) to undo.

## What it doesn't do

Arrange works with the site's own sections. It doesn't change spacing, columns or sizes, and it doesn't invent new kinds of section — a developer adds those (or the assistant, in **Build**), and they then appear in the panel.

## If something's different

- **"Sections can't be arranged on this page."** The site hasn't marked its sections for Arrange. Add, move and remove them in the [form](/docs/editing/form/) instead, and point the site's developer to [what Arrange needs](/docs/visual-builder/).
- **"This page couldn't be drawn."** The site ran into a problem drawing the page — usually a section just added that needs something filled in. The note names the change; **Undo** takes it back and the page draws again, or **Show the form** to fill the section in. Nothing is lost, and nothing goes live until you publish.
- **"The new … section starts with the video from the “Home” page."** A picture, video or file a section can't do without starts with one this site already uses. It's yours to change in the form.
- **An option switches back by itself.** The site refused the value; the panel says why.
