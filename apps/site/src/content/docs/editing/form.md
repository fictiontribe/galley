For people who edit a site. The form is the surest way to change anything on a page: every field the page has is there, labelled in the site's own words.

## Change a field

1. In the sidebar, open **Pages** (or the collection you want) and choose the page.
2. In the form on the left, find the field. A page built from sections lists them by name — **Campaign hero**, **Photo and text** — with their first words; click one to open its fields.
3. Type. The change is saved as you type: the field shows **Edited**, and **Proof** in the top bar counts one more change.

**Put back**, beside the field, returns it to what is published. The page in the middle shows the change a moment later.

A field marked **\*** must have a value. The small grey name beside the label of a page's own fields (`title`, `description`) is the field's name in the file, for whoever looks after the site's code.

## Sections

- **Move** a section with the up and down arrows on its row, or drag it by its handle.
- **⋯** on a section's row: **Add section above**, **Add section below**, **Duplicate**, **Remove section**.
- **Add section**, under the list, opens **Add a section**: choose a type and it goes at the end of the page, its layout taken from one already on the site and its words empty.

To arrange sections on the page itself, with pictures of each type, use [Arrange](/docs/editing/page-builder/).

## Longer text

The main text of a post or page has a toolbar for headings, bold, italic, links, lists, quotes and **Image from media**. The **Search & AI** panel at the top of the form holds the title and description search engines show.

## Make or delete a page

- **New page**, at the top of a collection, asks for a title, an address and a description, then **Create page**.
- **⋯** beside a page in the list › **Delete…**. The page goes when you publish; until then it can be put back in Proof.

## If something's different

- **"This file changed since you opened it, so your edit wasn't saved."** Someone (or the assistant) changed the same page at the same moment. Reopen the page and make the change again.
- **A field is missing from the form.** The site's developer can name it in the site's settings file ([Collections and fields](/docs/configuration/collections/)); until then, ask the assistant or use **Source**.
- **The page in the middle doesn't change.** Press **Reload the page** above it. If it says the preview stopped, see [When something goes wrong](/docs/editing/troubleshooting/).
