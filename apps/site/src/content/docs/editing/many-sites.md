For people who look after more than a handful of sites — an agency, a freelancer. One Galley keeps a list of all of them; each site stays in its own folder, with its own version history.

## Add your sites

On the **Sites** screen (**Your sites**), press **Add sites**:

- **Add sites from a folder…** — choose the folder your sites live in. Galley looks one and two levels down, lists everything that looks like a site, and adds the ones you tick. Nothing in their folders changes.
- **Open one folder…**, **Clone a site…** or **Create a new site…** for one at a time.

## File them by client

- **New group** names a client and gives it a colour.
- A site's **⋯** menu: **Move to group…**, **Pin to the top**, **Add a note…** ("Hesper is the contact; spring lookbook due in March"), **Details…**, **Remove from this app…** (its folder is never touched).

Groups, pins and notes are kept on this computer, not in any site.

## See what each is waiting on

Each card shows chips, most urgent first: **N changes** waiting in Proof, **N unread** form submissions, versions not sent or not live, **Folder missing**, **Preview running**. A site with nothing waiting says **Up to date**. **Needs attention** shows only the sites with something waiting; search finds sites by name, client, note or address.

## Switch between sites

The site's name at the top of the sidebar opens the switcher; **⌘K** (Ctrl+K) finds a site by name too. Each site remembers where you were in it.

## A client's site with its own keys

In a site's **Settings › Connections**, **Use a key of this site's own…** gives it its own assistant, image or hosting key, so that client is billed for their site's use and nothing else; **Use an author of this site's own…** sets the name its versions are published under. Each line says which key is in use and shows only its last four characters. **Settings › Assistant use** shows what the assistant has cost on that site.

## Keeping the computer quick

Each site's preview runs on this computer. **App settings › Previews** sets how many run at once (3 by default) and when an unused one stops (after 20 minutes by default). A stopped preview starts again when you come back.

## Not there yet

There are no user accounts or per-site permissions: anyone who can open this Galley can edit and publish every site in its list. Keys are kept in files only you can read, not yet in the system keychain. More: [Self-hosting](/docs/running/hosted/), [Security](/docs/running/security/).
