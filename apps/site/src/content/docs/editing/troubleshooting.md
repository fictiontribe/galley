For people who edit a site. First, your work is almost certainly safe: changes are kept in the site on this computer as you make them, not in the browser window. Closing the app, losing the connection or a failed publish doesn't lose them — they wait in Proof.

Many messages have a **Technical details** line. You don't need to read it, but copy it when you ask for help.

## The page in the middle is blank or stopped

The preview is your site running on this computer.

- **The preview is off.** Press **Start preview** (on Site home, or in the editor). It starts by itself when you open the editor.
- **Preparing the preview…** or **Starting the preview…** The first start can take a few minutes. Wait.
- **The preview stopped.** Press **Restart preview** once. If it stops again, a recent change to the site's design or settings is usually the cause: send the technical details to whoever looks after the site. You can keep editing in the form meanwhile.
- **Waking the preview…** Previews nobody has used for a while are stopped to keep the computer quick; it starts again by itself.

## A change didn't save

- **"This file changed since you opened it, so your edit wasn't saved."** Someone — or the assistant — changed the same page at that moment. Reopen the page and make the change again.
- **A field shows a red message under it.** It says what's wrong — a required field is empty, a link leads nowhere. Fix it there.

## Publishing didn't finish

- **"Couldn't publish."** Your changes are safe. The window says why; **Try again**.
- **"Saved, not live yet."** The version is saved here but didn't reach the site's host. **Send now** in the status bar sends it when the connection is back.
- **"The live site has newer changes."** Someone published from elsewhere. Press **Get the latest**, then publish again.

## The app itself

- **"Can't reach the app's server."** The part of the app that does the work stopped. Start it again (or ask whoever runs it), then **Try again**.
- **Live updates paused — reconnecting.** The connection dropped for a moment and comes back by itself.
- **"Sign in to continue."** On a hosted editor, sign in again with your e-mail and password ([Sign in to a hosted editor](/docs/editing/signing-in/)). On your own computer: **"Open the link printed when the app started to continue."**
