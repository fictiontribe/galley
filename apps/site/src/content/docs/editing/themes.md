For everyone who uses the app. A **theme** sets the app's paper, ink and pencil colors and a few materials: whether the sidebar is paper, a toned desk or a dark plate, and how far things stand off the page. **Light or dark** is separate: that's the appearance, the switch beside the theme picker. Themes change the app only, never your site.

*Status: new, in final checks. Quiet, Plate and Desk come with the app; you can make your own and share it.*

## Choose one

1. At the foot of the sidebar (or the top corner of the sign-in screen), press the small swatch beside the sun or moon.
2. Every theme is drawn as a swatch with its name. Choose one: the app changes at once.
3. **Browse community themes** lists the ones other people made. They come with the app, so nothing is downloaded.

On your own computer, the theme is the app's. On an editor your team shares, each person chooses their own, kept in their browser; until you choose, you see the one the editor's owner set for someone new.

## Make one

1. Open **Settings › Themes** (or **Make a theme…** in the picker).
2. Choose **Make a theme from this one** beside the theme closest to what you want.
3. Change the five colors with the color picker or by typing a value:
   - **Paper**: the ground everything sits on.
   - **Ink**: text, and every quieter text, line and edge.
   - **Pencil**: the one action, where you are, focus and the assistant.
   - **Plate**: the dark sidebar, when the sidebar is a plate.
   - **Table**: the ground under a page while you proof it.
4. Dark colors are made from yours. To set them yourself, turn on **Set dark colors myself**.
5. Choose the sidebar, the assistant's look, the icons and the depth. Each choice is drawn small.

The whole app shows your theme as you edit, in your browser only, until you save. **Undo last change** steps back one change; leaving with unsaved changes asks first.

## Readable, always

The app checks every theme before it's shown or saved. If a color would make something hard to read, it moves that color a little and says so, beside the color and under **Readability**: *"Your paper was too dark for a light theme, so it was lightened from #CFCBC4 to #ECE8E0."* It also tells you when your pencil is close to a color that already means something, like the green of an insertion. A theme that had to be adjusted is saved with the adjusted colors. **Technical details** lists every pair it measured.

The colors that mean something (insertions, deletions, changes, warnings, the colors that mark people) aren't part of a theme; the check may only adjust how light they are.

## Save it

- On your own computer: **Save**. It's kept with the app's settings, never in a site.
- On a shared editor: **Save for me** keeps it in this browser. People who can manage the editor also have **Save for everyone here**, and can make a theme the one someone new sees (**Make it the default**, or **App settings › Theme for someone new**).

In **Settings › Themes** you can use, edit, duplicate, export or delete the themes you saved.

## Share it

- **Export** downloads the theme as a small file. **Import…** reads one back; the app checks it and shows you the report before you save it.
- **Share with the community…** opens GitHub in a new tab with your theme filled in as a new file in the public themes folder. You propose it there with your own GitHub account; once it's accepted, it comes with the next version for everyone. The app sends nothing itself and needs no account of its own.
