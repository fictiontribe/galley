For people who edit a site. When you can see the words you want to change, clicking them is quicker than finding their field.

## Retype text where it sits

1. Open the page in the editor. Above the page, **Click to edit** is chosen.
2. Point at the words. They get a dotted outline and a **Click to edit** tag.

<figure class="frame" style="padding: clamp(12px, 3vw, 28px); margin-block: 0 1.15em"><img src="/docs/on-the-page-click-to-edit@1x.webp" srcset="/docs/on-the-page-click-to-edit@1x.webp 1x, /docs/on-the-page-click-to-edit.webp 2x" alt="The Caddow home page in the editor with Click to edit chosen: the line under the big heading has a dotted blue outline and a blue Click to edit tag above it." width="756" height="547" loading="lazy" decoding="async"></figure>

3. Click, and type over the words.
4. Press **Enter** to keep the change, or **Escape** to leave the words as they were.

The change is saved like any edit in the form: the field shows **Edited** and Proof counts it. Pictures say **Click to change** instead, and open **Choose an image** with the site's media.

To follow a link without editing it, use the **Follow link** button on its tag, or choose **Browse** above the page.

## When it asks "Which one did you mean?"

Some words come from more than one place — the same menu item in the header and in the footer. Galley won't guess:

<figure class="frame" style="padding: clamp(12px, 3vw, 28px); margin-block: 0 1.15em"><img src="/docs/on-the-page-which-one@1x.webp" srcset="/docs/on-the-page-which-one@1x.webp 1x, /docs/on-the-page-which-one.webp 2x" alt="A small window under the word Atelier in the Caddow footer, titled Which one did you mean?, saying the text appears in 2 places and listing Navigation › Header › 2 › Label and Navigation › Footer › 1 › Links › 2 › Label." width="429" height="267" loading="lazy" decoding="async"></figure>

Choose the one you meant, then type.

## If something's different

- **Nothing outlines when you point.** Make sure **Click to edit** is chosen, and that the page has finished loading.
- **"This text is part of the site's design."** It isn't stored with the page's content — it's in the site's design, or worked out from other details (a date, a reading time). Ask the assistant in **Build** mode, or the site's developer.
- **A picture "isn't one of this page's own image fields".** Change it in the [form](/docs/editing/form/), in its section's image field.
